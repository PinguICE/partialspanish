SECTION 1 · READING COMPREHENSION

Leo's exchange year

1 / 5

When Leo moved abroad for his exchange year, he initially felt incredibly homesick and lonely because he missed his family and familiar surroundings. One reason for his struggle was that he suffered from severe insomnia, unable to fall asleep on his firm new mattress. Every night, he would lie awake listening to his roommate snore, which only served to keep him awake even longer. Due to his lack of sleep, Leo became extremely grumpy and irritated during morning classes, often nodding off at his desk. As a result, he worried he would oversleep and miss important exams.

However, things changed whenever he joined the school theater group. Whoever spoke to Leo noticed a dramatic transformation; he appeared much more cheerful and grateful for the opportunity. Over time, he learned to get used to his new schedule. Now, wherever he goes on campus, he feels thrilled and relieved to have adapted so well.

Critical Analysis — responde en 1–2 oraciones
Explain how Leo's experience shows the transition from feeling overwhelmed to getting used to a new environment.
Possible answer:

At first, Leo felt homesick and lonely, and his insomnia affected his mood at school. After joining the theater group, he became more cheerful and gradually got used to his new schedule.

SECTION 6 · SHORT WRITING TASK

Write your paragraph

80–120 words

Describe una situación donde un estudiante enfrenta un problema relacionado con el sueño o el estrés antes de un evento importante. Explica qué pasó y cómo se resolvió.

Requisitos obligatorios del worksheet
• Al menos 3 palabras: anxious, fast asleep, furious, oversleep, insomnia, relieved, envious.
• Al menos 2 estructuras de causa/efecto: because of, consequently, leads to, since, as a result.
0 palabrasMeta: 80–120 palabras
Ejemplo modelo (90 palabras):

Ana felt anxious before her final exam because of her insomnia. She stayed awake studying and was afraid she would oversleep. The lack of rest led to poor concentration, and consequently she made mistakes during practice. Her friend helped her organize a study schedule and encouraged her to rest. That night, Ana finally slept and woke up on time. She felt relieved because she was prepared. Although she had been stressed, she learned that good sleep habits can make a big difference before an important event.
